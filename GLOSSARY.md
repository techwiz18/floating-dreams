# Floating Dreams

A small, pressureless browser sky: you drift an orb around, gather crystals, and
plant what you gather into a garden that stays.

## Language

**Orb**:
The glowing thing the player steers. It is the player.
_Avoid_: Player, avatar, ship, cursor

**Sky**:
The whole playable space, and the round area within it that the orb cannot leave.
_Avoid_: Level, map, world, arena

**Crystal**:
A floating octahedron the orb gathers by touching it. There are always crystals
to gather; gathering one makes another appear elsewhere.
_Avoid_: Gem, shard, orb, pickup

**Dream**:
The lifetime count of crystals gathered. A dream is never spent or lost.
_Avoid_: Score, points, count

**Bloom**:
What a planted dream becomes. Blooms stay where they are planted, permanently.
_Avoid_: Flower, plant, seedling, tree
